A tile in Power BI says 42,000. It looks like a fact. It is the end of a
trip. One row made that trip through eight stops, and at each stop something
about it changed. Some of the changes were decisions. Some were defaults.

This post follows one order from the checkout to the tile. Scroll through
the eight stops. The row on the rail is the order.

## Walk one row back

When two dashboards disagree, the cause is almost always one stop where they
made a different choice. Local time or UTC. Cents or euros. Paid orders or
all orders. The fastest way to find it is to take one row and walk it back,
stop by stop, until the two paths split.

The month of this order is a good example. The app says September. The
warehouse says August. Both are correct for their own clock. The report must
say which clock it uses. If it does not, the number is not wrong, but it is
not a fact either.
