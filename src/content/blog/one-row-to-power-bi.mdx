## Tracing a difference between reports

Two dashboards can disagree because they use different time zones, units,
or filters. Following a single row through both pipelines can help locate
the difference.

Here, the customer paid at 00:30 on September 1 in Lisbon, which was still
August 31 in UTC. The app records a September purchase, while the warehouse
includes it in August. Both dates describe the same event. The report needs
to state which time zone defines its reporting month.
